Field-by-Field Reference — Form N

(1)Serial No. — auto-increment, never reset mid-year
(2)Date — every calendar day required, even zero-movement days
(3)Fertilizer name + grade (NPK % e.g. 18:46:00) — from product master
(4)Opening = prior day's closing; first entry uses physical count
(5)Receipts = quantity + supplier name + supplier invoice no.
(6)Form O reference no. from supplier — mandatory per FCO for every receipt
(7)Total Available = Opening Stock + Receipts (computed)
(8)Sales = sum of all bills for that product on that date
(9)Damage / adjustment — note reason in remarks column
(10)Closing = Total Available − Sales − Damage; must match physical stock
(11)Remarks — inspector notes, quality issues, shortages, returns

Legal Consequence Every insecticide sale must be entered immediately in Form XII. Selling without entry, selling expired stock, or selling banned pesticides = up to 2 years imprisonment + fine + license cancellation under the Insecticides Act 1968.
